# Chapters Directory

Each manuscript chapter has its own dedicated folder under `draft/chapters/`.

## Naming Convention

`CH01_working-title/`

Chapter numbers and working titles are provisional. Folders may be renamed or reordered once the book architecture is approved by collaborators. Do not treat folder names or sequence as an approved table of contents.

## Required Files Per Chapter Folder

- `chapter-brief.md` — Chapter objective, scope, central argument, relationship to the book, and current status.
- `outline.md` — Structured chapter outline following `draft/CHAPTER_TEMPLATE.md`.
- `research-notes.md` — Literature findings, arguments, evidence, analytical observations.
- `draft.md` — Canonical working manuscript prose for the chapter.
- `sources-used.md` — Source IDs, bibliographic references, page numbers, verification status.
- `editorial-review.md` — Human feedback, revision notes, unresolved issues, editorial decisions.
- `figures/` — Chapter-specific diagrams, graphs, charts, and visual materials.

## Chapter Status Values

Use exactly one status in `chapter-brief.md`:

- PLANNING
- RESEARCHING
- OUTLINING
- DRAFTING
- FOR HUMAN REVIEW
- REVISION
- APPROVED

A chapter is not finalized without human approval.

## Source of Truth

`draft.md` inside each chapter folder is the canonical working manuscript for that chapter. Do not maintain multiple competing versions. A consolidated manuscript is a compiled output of individual chapter drafts; preserve original chapter files.

## Figures

Every figure must include title, caption, source, methodology, and limitations. Store original datasets separately in `research/`. Never fabricate statistical values or visualizations.

## Academic Writing Standards

- Formal academic English, natural readable prose.
- Analytical, evidence-based arguments.
- Consistent scholarly voice across chapters.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon and repetitive AI-style transitions.
- No fabricated references or unsupported claims.
- No em dashes in manuscript prose.
- APA 7 author-date citations provisionally, pending confirmation of final citation style.
