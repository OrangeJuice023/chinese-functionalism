# PHASE 2 REPORT — Independent Research Audit and Theoretical Critique

## 1. Executive Summary
Phase 2 audited the existing research without expanding the manuscript. It identified bibliographic, theoretical, and empirical weaknesses, verified sources at metadata level, and produced competing conceptual formulations. The evidence does not yet establish Chinese Functionalism as a distinct theory. It remains a provisional analytical framework requiring further testing.

## 2. Major Findings
- 18 sources registered; metadata verified; full-text review pending for most.
- Several bibliographic items require verification (SRC-0005, SRC-0012, SRC-0015, SRC-0017).
- Working framework conflates assumptions with findings.
- Chinese IR theories are not functionalist; should not be conflated.
- China-ASEAN cooperation is extensive but coexists with competition and asymmetry.

## 3. Critical Weaknesses in Original Research
- Overgeneralization from limited cases.
- Missing counterarguments (coercion, failure, spillback).
- Assumptions presented as findings.
- Incomplete citation verification.

## 4. Bibliographic Corrections
- SRC-0005 DOI approximate; verify.
- SRC-0017 citation incomplete.
- SRC-0012 chapter attribution verify.
- SRC-0015 verify quote attribution.

## 5. Theoretical Findings
- Classical functionalism is not apolitical; Mitrany drew on pluralist theory.
- Neofunctionalism's spillover mechanisms are well-defined but criticized.
- Chinese IR theories (relational, moral realism, Tianxia, symbiosis) are not functionalist.
- Alternative frameworks (economic statecraft, complex interdependence, liberal institutionalism) may suffice.

## 6. Empirical Findings
- Functional cooperation extensive in trade, infrastructure, digital, energy, institutions.
- Coexists with maritime disputes and power asymmetry.
- Smaller states hedge and resist.
- Agreements do not equal success.

## 7. Revised Conceptual Possibilities
- Formulation A (modified functionalism) vs Formulation B (analytical adaptation).
- Recommended default: Formulation B, with A as competing hypothesis.

## 8. Outstanding Research Gaps
- Full-text reviews needed.
- Outcome-level evidence needed.
- Philippine case study needed.
- Falsification testing needed.
