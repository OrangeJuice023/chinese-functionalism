# Phase 2 Audit — Chinese Functionalism Research

## Scope
Audit of repository state at commit 959cca3. Reviewed: WORKING_DRAFT_v0.1.md, source-register, annotated-bibliography, sources-to-review, evidence-matrix, working-framework, comparative-analysis.

## Findings

### 1. Unsupported or weakly supported claims
- Location: WORKING_DRAFT_v0.1.md, Part II, trade figures (USD 913.1B, USD 26.2B FDI). Severity: Medium. Source: ASEAN Secretariat 2026 overview. Correction: figures are preliminary official statistics; independently verify via ASEAN stats and mark as provisional.
- Location: Part II, "China has been ASEAN's largest trading partner for eighteen consecutive years." Severity: Low. Supported by ASEAN overview but verify definition of "trading partner" (goods only vs goods+services).
- Location: Part III, claim that no Chinese IR theory is explicitly functionalist. Severity: Medium. Based on literature search; requires systematic review of Chinese-language scholarship. Mark as provisional.

### 2. Bibliographic issues
- SRC-0005 (Ruggie 2005): DOI not verified. The cited DOI 10.1146/annurev.polisci.8.082103.104849 is approximate. Correction: verify exact DOI via Annual Reviews.
- SRC-0017 (Zhang Feng 2015): citation incomplete (no journal volume/pages). Correction: verify full citation.
- SRC-0012 (Chen ed. 2024): chapter-level attribution; verify chapter author and page range.
- SRC-0015 (Moravcsik 2005): DOI provided resolves to Journal of European Public Policy article on European constitutional compromise; verify it contains the neofunctionalist critique attributed.

### 3. Citations not directly supporting statements
- Moravcsik (2005) cited for "over ambitious, one sided, unfalsifiable" critique. The quote appears in secondary summaries; verify against original text.
- "Mitrany and the Emergence of the Functional Approach (2017)" cited for Mitrany as catalyst. Verify chapter content.

### 4. Overgeneralizations
- "China's engagement with ASEAN exhibits extensive functional cooperation" — broad; needs sector-by-sector evidence.
- Smaller states "navigate asymmetry through hedging" — Kuik 2008 covers Malaysia/Singapore only; generalization to all Southeast Asian states unsupported.

### 5. Missing theoretical debates
- No discussion of Moravcsik's liberal intergovernmentalism as alternative.
- No discussion of economic statecraft literature.
- No engagement with Chinese School debates beyond naming theories.
- No discussion of spillback/failure cases in China-ASEAN cooperation.

### 6. Missing counterarguments
- Framework does not address coercion, failed cooperation, or exit by smaller states.
- No counterargument that functional cooperation may be purely transactional.

### 7. Assumptions presented as findings
- "Functional cooperation can generate interdependence and institutional habits" in working-framework is an assumption, not a verified finding.
- Provisional findings in Closing section overstate evidence base.

## Recommendations
- Verify all DOIs and citations before Draft v0.2.
- Add counterarguments and alternative explanations.
- Conduct full-text reviews.
- Refine framework with falsification criteria (done in working-framework, needs expansion).
- Do not promote sources to APPROVED.
