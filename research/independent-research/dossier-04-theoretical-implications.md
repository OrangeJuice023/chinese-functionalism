# Dossier 04 — Theoretical Implications

## Chinese School Debate
The Chinese School is plural and contested, not a unified theory. Relational theory, moral realism, Tianxia, and symbiosis theory are distinct approaches, none functionalist.

## Functionalism in Chinese IR
Functionalism appears as an established analytical approach used to interpret international organizations and cooperation. It is not a distinct Chinese school.

## Chinese Functionalism Status
No evidence of an established "Chinese Functionalism" theory. The concept is a project-proposed analytical lens. The word "Chinese" may refer to empirical focus rather than intellectual origin.
