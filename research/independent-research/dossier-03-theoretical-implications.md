# Dossier 03 — Theoretical Implications

Maritime evidence qualifies rather than confirms functionalist claims.

- Classical functionalism: functional needs present, but state authority central; technical tasks not insulated from politics.
- Neofunctionalism: limited automatic spillover; cooperation compartmentalized.
- Liberal institutionalism/intergovernmentalism: strong fit for institutionalized dialogue and negotiated arrangements.
- Economic statecraft/security studies: essential for coercion and strategic competition.
- Complex interdependence: multiple channels present but vulnerabilities persist.
- International law: legal entitlements shape cooperation scope.

Formulation B remains provisional. Formulation A challenged by state primacy and compartmentalization. Distinctiveness unproven.
