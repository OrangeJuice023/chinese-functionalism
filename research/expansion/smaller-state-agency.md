# Smaller-State Agency

Smaller states do not merely receive China's functional cooperation; they negotiate, resist, and redirect it. Responses vary by state capacity, economic structure, and security interests.

- Malaysia: hedging; engagement with diversification.
- Singapore: selective engagement; high capacity; institutional leadership.
- Vietnam: cooperation with strong resistance on maritime issues.
- Indonesia: renegotiation of terms; infrastructure acceptance with conditions.
- Laos: high dependence; limited bargaining leverage.
- Cambodia: close alignment; possible patron-client dynamics.
- Philippines: economic engagement constrained by maritime disputes and alliance politics.

Do not assume hedging applies equally. Smaller-state agency is a key test for the proposed framework: if cooperation is purely top-down, functionalism's emphasis on mutual needs is weakened.
