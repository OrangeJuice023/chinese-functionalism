# Phase 3 Framework Assessment

## Formulation B (Analytical Adaptation)
Remains defensible as a heuristic for identifying functional cooperation under asymmetry. Modest claims; falsifiable.

## Formulation A (Modified Functionalism)
Deserves further development only if evidence shows state-led functional cooperation produces institutional effects beyond economic statecraft.

## Rejection Option
If case studies show cooperation is purely transactional with no spillover or institutional binding, consider dropping "functionalism" and reframing as economic statecraft/interdependence.

## Retain Title?
Provisional yes, with explicit caveat that Chinese Functionalism is a proposed analytical lens, not an established theory.

## Conclusion
Evidence supports functional cooperation existence; distinctiveness unproven. Recommend continuing with Formulation B while testing against negative cases.
