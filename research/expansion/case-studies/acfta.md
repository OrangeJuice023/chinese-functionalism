# Case Study: ASEAN-China Free Trade Area (ACFTA)

## Background
ACFTA signed 2002, realized 2010. Upgraded to ACFTA 3.0 with digital economy, green economy, supply chain connectivity, consumer protection, MSMEs (ASEAN 2024).

## Actors
ASEAN member states and China.

## Objectives
Reduce tariffs, expand trade and investment, enhance economic integration.

## Implementation
Tariff reduction schedules, rules of origin, economic and technical cooperation.

## Outcomes
Trade growth; China largest ASEAN partner; preliminary 2025 trade USD 913.1B (ASEAN 2026).

## Costs/Benefits
Mutual market access; ASEAN concerns about trade imbalances and competition.

## Interdependence
High and growing.

## Asymmetry
China's larger market; ASEAN dependence.

## Smaller-State Agency
ASEAN negotiated upgrades; sought safeguards.

## Resistance/Failure
Trade imbalances, non-tariff barriers, implementation gaps.

## Alternative Explanations
Comparative advantage, global value chains, institutionalism.

## Framework Assessment
Supports functional cooperation existence; does not prove functionalist doctrine. Consistent with both economic statecraft and interdependence.
