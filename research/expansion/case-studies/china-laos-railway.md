# Case Study: China-Laos Railway

## Background
Completed railway connecting Kunming to Vientiane under BRI.

## Actors
China and Laos.

## Objectives
Connectivity, trade, regional integration.

## Implementation
Chinese financing and construction; Lao participation.

## Outcomes
Operational railway; increased connectivity.

## Costs/Benefits
Laos gains connectivity and debt; China gains access and influence.

## Interdependence
High for Laos (debt, trade route).

## Asymmetry
Strong: Chinese financing, contractors, standards.

## Smaller-State Agency
Laos accepted terms; limited renegotiation leverage.

## Resistance/Failure
Debt sustainability concerns; local displacement.

## Alternative Explanations
Development finance, infrastructure gap filling, economic statecraft.

## Framework Assessment
Functional cooperation present but heavily asymmetric; challenges functionalist optimism about mutual benefit.
