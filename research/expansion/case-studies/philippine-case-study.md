# Philippine Case Study

## Overview
The Philippines combines economic engagement with China, maritime disputes, ASEAN diplomacy, and security partnerships (US). Functional cooperation and strategic competition coexist.

## Economic Engagement
Trade and investment with China; infrastructure proposals; development finance. Implementation varies by administration.

## Maritime Disputes
South China Sea tensions constrain cooperation; incidents affect trust.

## ASEAN Diplomacy
Philippines participates in ASEAN mechanisms; seeks multilateral approaches.

## Security Partnerships
US alliance and other partnerships provide security insurance.

## Domestic Politics
Policy shifts across administrations; public opinion on China varies.

## Functional Cooperation vs Competition
Cooperation possible in trade, infrastructure, disaster management; overridden by maritime security concerns.

## Smaller-State Agency
Philippines negotiates, resists, and diversifies partnerships.

## Assessment
Functional cooperation does not eliminate strategic competition. Philippine case challenges any framework assuming automatic spillover from economic to political cooperation.
