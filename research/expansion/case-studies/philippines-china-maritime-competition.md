# Case Study: Philippines-China Maritime Competition

## Background
Philippines-China maritime relations combine functional cooperation (BCM, RoRe arrangement) with persistent disputes and reported incidents.

## Actors
Philippines and China.

## Objectives
Dispute management, practical cooperation, sovereignty protection.

## Implementation
BCM (2017), provisional RoRe arrangement (July 2024).

## Outcomes
13 resupply missions without dangerous encounters (to 4 Mar 2026 per AMTI); reported interference 24 Sep 2026.

## Costs/Benefits
Temporary operational accommodation; no broader settlement.

## Interdependence
Limited in maritime domain.

## Asymmetry
High.

## Smaller-State Agency
Philippines uses diplomacy, law, public communication, alliance partnerships.

## Resistance/Failure
Renewed incidents; arrangement narrow and reversible.

## Alternative Explanations
Security studies, international law, economic statecraft.

## Framework Assessment
Challenges automatic spillover; supports compartmentalized cooperation.
