# Case Study: South China Sea Maritime Cooperation

## Background
ASEAN and China adopted the DOC in 2002, identifying areas for practical cooperation (marine environmental protection, marine scientific research, navigation safety, search and rescue, transnational crime).

## Actors
ASEAN member states and China.

## Objectives
Confidence-building, practical cooperation, COC negotiation.

## Implementation
Recurring SOM-DOC, JWG-DOC meetings, workshops, training.

## Outcomes
Institutionalized dialogue; COC under negotiation as of Nov 2026.

## Costs/Benefits
Dialogue channels maintained; limited project-level outputs documented.

## Interdependence
Limited; cooperation compartmentalized.

## Asymmetry
High; power asymmetry shapes terms.

## Smaller-State Agency
ASEAN institutional engagement; Philippines legal and diplomatic strategies.

## Resistance/Failure
COC not concluded; maritime incidents reported.

## Alternative Explanations
Institutionalism, intergovernmentalism, security studies.

## Framework Assessment
Supports functional need but challenges automatic spillover. Cooperation compartmentalized.
