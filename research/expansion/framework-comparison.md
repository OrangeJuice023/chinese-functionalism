# Framework Comparison Matrix

| Framework | Unit of Analysis | Assumptions | Mechanisms | Explanatory Scope | Limitations | Observable Implications |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Classical Functionalism | Functional agencies/needs | Technical tasks separable from politics | Functional cooperation, technical self-determination | Sectoral cooperation, peace | Ignores power, assumes de-politicization | Sectoral agencies, cross-border bonds |
| Neofunctionalism | Regional integration process | Spillover automaticity | Functional/political/cultivated spillover, spillback | European integration | Over-prediction, elite bias, unfalsifiable | Policy expansion, loyalty transfer |
| Liberal Institutionalism | Institutions/states | Shared interests, anarchy | Transaction cost reduction, information | Cooperation under anarchy | Underweights power asymmetry | Institutional participation |
| Liberal Intergovernmentalism | States | State preferences central | Bargaining, delegation | Regional integration | Underweights supranational dynamics | State-led agreements |
| Economic Statecraft | States | Economic tools serve strategy | Incentives, coercion, leverage | Trade/investment/finance | May overstate intentionality | Trade dependence, coercion episodes |
| Complex Interdependence | States/societies | Multiple channels, reduced force | Interdependence, issue linkage | Economic relationships | Understates coercion | Cross-border flows |
| Strategic Regionalism | Regions/states | Geopolitics shapes cooperation | Strategic competition, hedging | Regional order | May overstate security logic | Alliance patterns, hedging |
| Chinese Functionalism (A) | State-led functional cooperation | State primacy + functional logic | Sectoral cooperation, interdependence, asymmetry | China-ASEAN practice | May be relabeling | Sectoral agreements, asymmetry |
| Chinese Functionalism (B) | Analytical heuristic | Descriptive patterns | Identify functional cooperation + power | Comparative cases | Modest, may add little | Observable sectoral patterns |
