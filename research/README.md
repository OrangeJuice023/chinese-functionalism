# Research Directory

Intellectual foundation development for the Chinese Functionalism project.

## Subdirectories

- `theoretical-foundations/` — Functionalism, neofunctionalism, regional integration, institutionalism.
- `conceptual-framework/` — Definition, operationalization, and critical evaluation of Chinese Functionalism.
- `thematic-reviews/` — Literature reviews organized by theme.
- `country-and-regional-studies/` — China, ASEAN, Philippines, Southeast Asia.
- `research-questions/` — Living document of research questions, hypotheses, gaps.
