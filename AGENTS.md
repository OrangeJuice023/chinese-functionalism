# AGENTS.md — Multi-Agent Operating Rules

Every AI agent working in this repository must follow these rules.

## Mandatory First Steps

1. Read `NORTH_STAR.md` first.
2. Inspect relevant research materials before making claims.
3. Review existing drafts before editing.
4. Check `progress/` for current status.

## Operating Rules

- Use consistent source IDs (SRC-0001, SRC-0002, etc.).
- Preserve original materials. Do not replace source documents with AI summaries.
- Avoid fabricating citations, references, page numbers, quotations, or source links.
- Distinguish source findings from analytical interpretations.
- Record unresolved issues.
- Update the appropriate progress checklist after completing work.
- Document significant modifications in `progress/logs/research-log.md`.
- Avoid independently changing the book's central argument.
- Do not change conceptual direction without human approval.
- Mark provisional content with `[PROVISIONAL — REQUIRES HUMAN REVIEW]`.

## Source Handling

- Do not claim a document has been reviewed unless its contents have actually been inspected.
- Do not confuse territorial sovereignty, sovereign rights, maritime entitlements, and maritime boundary delimitation.
- Avoid simplistic pro-China or anti-China narratives.

## Task Completion Report

When an agent completes a task, it must provide:

1. Task completed.
2. Files created.
3. Files modified.
4. Sources reviewed.
5. Main findings.
6. Outstanding questions.
7. Recommended next steps.

## Prohibited Actions

- Do not silently rewrite major sections.
- Do not change the project's conceptual direction.
- Do not fabricate or invent sources.
- Do not overwrite existing Git history.
- Do not force-push.
- Do not publish private or copyrighted materials without authorization.
- Do not assume missing data is zero or fabricate values.
